# Project Log

## Early November 2026 — Choosing My Topic

When I was trying to find a topic for my DSA210 project,I wanted to use something from my own life instead of just finding a dataset online.

I've been tracking my sleep on my iPhone for years but I never actually looked into the data. At first I was thinking about doing something with my Screen Time especially TikTok and Instagram. Then I thought about my class schedule and how different my days are depending on when I have classes.

This semester I'm taking IE305, IE311, IE451, MATH204 and DSA210. Some days I have to wake up pretty early like Wednesdays when my first class starts at 8:40 AM. Other days I can sleep longer. And when exams come up, my schedule gets even more irregular.

I wanted to see if I could actually notice these differences in my sleep records.That's how I ended up choosing this topic.

## Mid-November 2026 — Looking at My Data

I chose September 28, 2026 as the starting date because that's when school started.I have older sleep data too, but I thought it would be easier to start with the fall semester since I already have my class and exam schedules.

I started looking through my iPhone sleep records and checking them against my academic calendar.

November was quite busy with exams. I had IE451, MATH204, IE311, IE305 and DSA210 exams, sometimes pretty close to each other.I also have MATH204 on December 5 and IE311 on December 13.I want to see if there's any difference in how I sleep before these exams compared to other days.

While going through my records I actually noticed that I had more than one entry for some dates.I decided to take the last entry shown for each of those days instead of counting them twice. I also noticed a few missing days that I'll have to keep in mind during the analysis.

I checked my Screen Time as well, but most of the data I currently have is weekly. I'm still not sure how useful it will be when I'm working with daily sleep records.
