# SleepOps
### Understanding Sleep Patterns Through Academic Schedules and Daily Habits

## 1. About the Project

I've been tracking my sleep and wake-up times on my iPhone for several years, mostly out of curiosity. This semester, I realized how much my daily routine changes depending on my university schedule. On some days, I have classes starting as early as 8:40 AM, while on others I can sleep much later. Exam weeks also make my schedule quite different from a normal week.

Since I already have a collection of my own sleep records, I thought it would be interesting to see whether these changes in my daily life are reflected in my sleeping habits.

For my DSA210 Introduction to Data Science project at Sabancı University, I decided to combine my sleep records with my academic calendar and phone usage data to explore these relationships.

## 2. Research Questions

The main goal of this project is to understand how my academic schedule and daily habits are related to my sleep patterns.

I want to investigate three questions:

1. How are early morning classes associated with my sleep timing and duration?
2. Do my sleeping habits change during exam periods?
3. Is there a relationship between my smartphone usage and sleep patterns?

I also want to explore whether information about my academic schedule can help predict my sleep timing.

## 3. Data Sources

I'm using data from my own daily life:

- **Sleep Records:** Sleep and wake-up times recorded through my iPhone's sleep settings. I manually record these times when I go to sleep and wake up.
- **Academic Schedule:** My weekly class timetable at Sabancı University.
- **Exam Calendar:** Dates of my midterms and final exams.
- **Screen Time:** Available iPhone usage statistics, including social media applications such as TikTok and Instagram.

My sleep records are self-tracked rather than measured with a wearable device, so they represent approximate sleep intervals.

The main analysis will focus on the Fall 2026 semester, starting on September 28, 2026.

## 4. Planned Analysis

I plan to use Python to organize and analyze the collected data.

First, I will clean the dataset by checking for missing dates, duplicate entries, and possible inconsistencies. Then, I will use exploratory data analysis and visualizations to understand how my sleep patterns change throughout the semester.

I also plan to test whether there are meaningful differences between regular school days, early class days, and exam periods.

If the available data is sufficient, I will explore simple predictive models and compare their performance.

## 5. Current Progress

So far, I have decided on my research questions and gathered my initial sleep records, class schedule, exam dates, and available Screen Time summaries.

I have also started organizing the data and identifying duplicate sleep records that need to be handled before the analysis.

My next step is to prepare a clean dataset and begin exploratory data analysis.

I will continue updating this repository as the project develops.
